# Dr. Atdhe Buja

**Cybersecurity Professor | CISO | Researcher | Author | Speaker**

I work at the intersection of **cybersecurity, digital forensics, IIoT/IoT security, SOC operations, and applied cyber education**. My work focuses on building practical cybersecurity laboratories, research datasets, cyber-physical testbeds, and machine learning–supported security models for education, research, and industry collaboration.

## Research and Technical Focus

- Artificial Intelligence agentic engineering setup design
- Digital forensics and incident response
- SOC lab design, threat intelligence, and case management
- Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) and cyber-physical security
- Machine learning for anomaly detection and threat detection
- Cybersecurity education, curriculum design, and hands-on labs
